ABOUT E-CLUB

Where curiosity turns into a venture

E-Club is Parul University's student entrepreneurship community. We help students explore innovation, leadership, and startups — moving from a first idea to a first pitch, project, or venture, alongside mentors, workshops, and peers who build with you.

MISSION & VISION

Building the future, together

Our mission is to foster a culture of innovation and problem-solving. We envision a community where every student has the resources, mentorship, and network to turn their boldest ideas into successful ventures.

ENTREPRENEURSHIP CLUB
CODE OF CONDUCT

Rules & Guidelines

As members and participants of E-Club, students adhere to ethical, professional, and collaborative standards:

  • Professional Conduct: Maintain respectful, ethical, and professional behavior across all meetings, events, and forums.
  • Responsible Participation: Actively engage and deliver on committed team tasks, projects, and club initiatives.
  • Mutual Respect: Value diverse perspectives and foster an inclusive environment where all ideas are welcomed and celebrated.
  • Event Guidelines: Adhere strictly to timelines, safety protocols, and organizer instructions during competitions, hackathons, and visits.
  • Resource Integrity: Do not misuse E-Club facilities, equipment, communication channels, or official club branding.
  • Confidentiality: Treat fellow founders’ startup ideas, proprietary discussions, and sensitive documentation with strict confidentiality.
  • Originality: Submit solely original projects, pitch decks, and work; respect intellectual property rights and avoid plagiarism.
  • Institutional Alignment: Abide by Parul University and PIERC disciplinary standards, code of conduct, and academic regulations.
ENTREPRENEURSHIP CLUB
DATA PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

E-Club and PIERC are committed to safeguarding the privacy and integrity of all student and founder information.

1. Information We Collect

Through our membership application and startup idea submission forms, we collect student names, email addresses, mobile numbers, academic credentials (student ID, department, year/semester), startup details (pitch decks, problem statements, team members), and interest areas.

2. Purpose of Collection

Collected information is utilized strictly for evaluating membership eligibility, coordinating club activities, facilitating 1-on-1 mentorship, event registration, and screening for PIERC incubation programs.

3. Data Protection & Confidentiality

All startup concepts and founder submissions are treated with strict confidentiality. We never sell, rent, or distribute student data to unauthorized third parties.
