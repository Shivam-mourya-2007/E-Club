ABOUT E-CLUB

The Digital Identity & Engagement Platform

E-Club is where students connect, ideas grow, and entrepreneurship begins.

💡

Explore Entrepreneurship

We provide a safe, inspiring environment for students of all departments to step outside textbooks, explore market opportunities, and understand the mechanics of building real-world ventures.

⚡

Foster Innovation & Leadership

Through collaborative sprints, hackathons, and high-stakes campus challenges, members develop hands-on problem-solving, rapid prototyping skills, and dynamic organizational leadership.

🌱

Startup Incubation Support

In collaboration with PIERC, students with validated ideas receive structured incubation, patent support, prototyping lab access, seed grants, and direct investor demo days.

OUR MISSION

To empower students to transform their ideas into meaningful projects and entrepreneurial opportunities.

We eliminate the friction between campus ideation and commercial execution by offering hands-on mentorship, technical tools, and a relentless peer ecosystem.

OUR VISION

To create a strong student entrepreneurship ecosystem that inspires innovation, leadership, and startup creation.

Our vision is to build a premier collegiate innovation pipeline recognized nationally for nurturing high-impact founders, scalable ventures, and game-changing solutions.

ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE

The 6 Major E-Club Teams

E-Club is run by dedicated student leaders organized into six specialized functional domains.

01

Chairperson

Provides overall leadership, strategic direction, coordination, and official representation of E-Club across the university, industry, and external partners.

02

Outreach

Handles corporate partnerships, ecosystem networking, external communications, mentor onboarding, founder relations, and ecosystem collaborations.

03

Technical

Manages the E-Club website, digital platforms, application portals, server architectures, form pipelines, and tech infrastructure support.

04

Management

Handles comprehensive event planning, venue coordination, operational logistics, resource allocation, task management, and flawless on-ground execution.

05

Documentation

Maintains official activity reports, meeting records, annual publications, event whitepapers, participant certifications, and archival documents.

06

Social Media

Drives social media presence, digital branding, promotional campaigns, event live-coverage, podcast interviews, and community engagement.

CODE OF CONDUCT

Rules & Guidelines

  • Maintain respectful and professional behavior at all times.
  • Participate responsibly and actively in club activities and team meetings.
  • Respect fellow members, their intellectual property, and their ideas.
  • Follow event rules, safety protocols, and organizational guidelines.
  • Do not misuse E-Club resources, prototyping labs, or official branding.
  • Maintain confidentiality of sensitive founder data and non-public pitch materials.
  • Submit only original ideas, genuine code, and authentic project content.
  • Adhere strictly to university institutional rules, codes of conduct, and policies.
DATA TRANSPARENCY

Privacy Policy

The E-Club website collects information through our membership and startup idea submission forms.

Collected information may include:

Full Name, email address, phone number, student ID / enrollment details, academic department, venture concepts, team member rosters, and uploaded pitch documents or demo URLs.

Legitimate Usage Guarantee:

All information collected is strictly utilized for legitimate E-Club activities such as cohort membership screening, club communications, hackathon scheduling, mentor matching, and PIERC incubation evaluation. Your intellectual property is never sold or shared with unauthorized third parties.
