About E-Club

ABOUT E-CLUB

Where curiosity turns into a venture

E-Club is Parul University's student entrepreneurship community. We help students explore innovation, leadership, and startups — moving from a first idea to a first pitch, project, or venture, alongside mentors, workshops, and peers who build with you.

Mission & Vision

MISSION & VISION

Building the future, together

Our mission is to foster a culture of innovation and problem-solving. We envision a community where every student has the resources, mentorship, and network to turn their boldest ideas into successful ventures.
